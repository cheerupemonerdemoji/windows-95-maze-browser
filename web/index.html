Privacy: your images stay in this browser and are never uploaded. A resized copy is kept in this browser's local storage (IndexedDB) so it comes back next time; use “Clear stored images” to delete it. Images that tile cleanly need to be seamless squares; other pictures are stretched to a square and will show seams. Themes only change the picture on the walls, floor and ceiling; the maze, timing and camera do not change.
